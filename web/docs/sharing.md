# Document sharing — SPA (v4.8.0, `web/`)

Moved out of `web/CLAUDE.md` so it is read on demand; `web/CLAUDE.md` keeps a mandatory-read pointer here.

The server rules live in `.claude/docs/features/sharing.md`; the SPA side: `api/shares.ts` (the typed client — the server derives each share's `link` and `status`, the client never rebuilds them), `utils/shareQueries.ts` (`invalidateShares` — the one place a share mutation invalidates from: every `["shares", …]` list, the document's own query and the bell), `components/ShareButton.tsx` + `ShareDialog.tsx` (people picker excluding the caller, deactivated accounts and people the caller already shares with; optional "Until" date; sequential POSTs with **itemized, retryable failures**; the document's current shares with Withdraw behind a confirm; Share is mounted in the `PageHeader` actions of the seven view pages — feedback, 1:1, goal, review, team KPI, impact entry, succession plan) and `components/SharedByBanner.tsx` ("Shared with you by …"; `role="status"` since v4.12.1 — informational, never an assertive alert). **Gate the Share button on the detail response's `canShare` only, never on `sharedBy`** (an upgraded read carries both). A sharee's view pages stay read-only by construction (their edit/transition/record gates key on author ids and server capability flags); two sharee-specific rules: `ViewOneOnOne` hides the per-action-item history icon (`sharedBy != null` — that route is own-right only and 403s sharees) and `ReviewSuccessionPlan` hides the development-goals field entirely (a share read always carries empty `goals`; "no linked goals" would be false). **The lapse 403** (problem `detail` "The person who shared this no longer has access to it", recognised by `utils/shareLapse.ts` — the detail phrase is the contract, the status is shared with the ordinary denial) renders the localized `sharing.lapsed` message on every view page. The **Shared screen** (`pages/Shares.tsx` at `/shares`, `?tab=withMe|byMe`, `SharesTable.tsx`; ungated nav leaf in My work with tour id `nav-shares` and a whirlwind stop) lists shares per tab with type/status filters (kinds whose feature the viewer disabled drop out of the type filter and, on "Shared by me", lose their Open action but stay withdrawable); the document label is built client-side from the `details` snapshot per kind (`utils/shareKinds.ts`, null details → "No longer available"). Share notifications are worded in `notifications.event.<area>Shared*`/`<area>ShareWithdrawn*` through context variants computed from the params (`until` / `author` / `sharer` / `owner` — see `SHARED_SPEC`/`SHARE_WITHDRAWN_SPEC` in `NotificationsButton.tsx`); the succession copies carry the sharer only, so their wording references nothing else. **Mass share (v4.10.0)**: the Team's-performance view's "Share reviews…" link (`ReviewsDashboard.tsx`, hidden on the HR auditor scope and while no period resolves) opens `pages/MassSharePerformanceReviews.tsx` at `/performance-reviews/mass-share?periodId=` — since v4.11.0 a thin per-kind page over the generic **`components/MassSharePage.tsx`** (since checkup #38 split into `hooks/useMassShareState.ts` — filters, paging and the selection — `MassShareFacets`, `MassShareSelectionBar`, `MassShareTable` + the memoized `MassShareTableRow`, and the prop types in `massShareTypes.ts`; the shell: header/back, MetaStrip, `ListToolbar` + the five people facets, selection buttons, the table with the six person columns — team names as linking `TeamBadges`, the inactive marker a `StatusPill`, since v4.12.1 — pagination, the dialog), which a kind configures with its `resourceType`/`kind`, `settingsKey`, candidate `rows`, `reasonText`, and optional `extraMeta`/`extraFilters`/`extraColumns` (the reviews page keeps its period parsing, the Status/Min-overall facets and the Status/Overall columns; the generic strings live in `sharing.massShare.*`, the review-only ones stay in `performanceReview.massShare.*`) (lazy, `PERFORMANCE_REVIEWS` page guard, a missing/malformed/stale period bounces to `/performance?tab=managed`; **no nav leaf and no tour anchor** — it is reached from the team view). It lists the `listShareCandidates` rows (view key `massShare`: filters, sort, page size — never the selection), narrowed by the pure, kind-generic `utils/massShare.ts` facets (a `MassShareRow` is `{person, resourceId, shareable, reason, review, teamNames, managerLabel}`; `buildReviewShareRows` is the reviews builder) (the direct-manager facet is a SUBTREE filter, labelled as such); unshareable people are greyed out with the reason as their checkbox's accessible name and are never selectable. The count shown and submitted is `selectedResourceIds(rows, selected).length`, never `selected.size`. `components/MassShareDialog.tsx` (props `resourceType` + `kind: "reviews" | "calendars"` — the kind is an i18next `context` over the `sharing.batch.*`/`sharing.toast.batchShared` texts, reviews reading the base keys, and the recipient-is-a-subject warning applies to reviews only) reuses `SharePeoplePicker` (`maxValues` = `MAX_BATCH_SHARE_SHAREES` 20), the single dialog's "Until" rules (shared since checkup #38: `hooks/useUntilDate.ts` + `components/UntilDateField.tsx`, and the 429-first error mapping `utils/shareErrors.ts`, each dialog passing its own key set) and `createShareBatches` (sequential ≤200-id chunks; it never throws — a rejected chunk comes back as `failure`, rendered as a partial result with "Retry the remaining N", a 429 with its own wording); the result panel groups ALREADY_SHARED per person (left unchanged, end date included) and feeds `summarizeBatchResult`/`retainUnsettled` the PRE-run rows snapshot, never the refetched candidates. **The calendars page (v4.11.0)** is the second kind: `pages/MassShareDaysOffCalendars.tsx` at `/days-off/mass-share` (lazy, `DAYS_OFF` page guard, settings key `massShare.daysOff`, back to `/days-off?tab=team`; no nav leaf, no tour anchor) is a thin composition over `MassSharePage` (`kind="calendars"`, `resourceType="DAYS_OFF_CALENDAR"`) — no extra facets/columns, no period; its rows come from `listDaysOffShareCandidates()` (`GET /days-off/share-candidates`, unpaged, the caller's chain) via `buildCalendarShareRows` (`resourceId` = the PERSON's user id, every row shareable, so nothing is greyed out), reached from the team tab's manager-only **"Share calendars…"** button (`daysOffMassShareLink()`). The wording is the i18next context `kindContext(kind)` (`utils/massShare.ts`, ONE helper shared by the page and the dialog; `MassShareKind` lives there too) over `sharing.batch.*`, `sharing.toast.batchShared` and `sharing.massShare.shareN` — each has `_calendars` variants (PL with `_few`/`_many`), and no "review" wording may appear on the calendars page (a test greps the EN and PL page and dialog). The recipient-is-a-subject warning stays reviews-only. Tests: `utils/shareKinds.test.ts` (also pins `REQUIRED_KEYS` against the spec's `ShareResponse.details` description), `MassSharePerformanceReviews.test.tsx`, `MassShareDaysOffCalendars.test.tsx`, `MassShareDialog.test.tsx`, `utils/massShare.test.ts`, the button cases in `ReviewsDashboard.test.tsx` and `DaysOff.test.tsx`.

**Days-off calendar shares (v4.11.0, the eighth kind)**: the resource is a PERSON (its id is the person's user id), there is no view page — the surfaces are the Days off page's Calendar tab and the bell/Shared/activity wording. **Sharee side**: the calendar's `Whose calendar` Select is now offered to EVERYONE (member + "Shared with me"; managed/managedAll for managers, org for the HR auditor), and `scope=shared` lists the people shared with the caller at teammate parity — the grid renders exactly what the API returns (a dimmed "Shared by {name}" line from `sharedBy`, no pool names, no budgets), with the empty copy `daysOff.calendar.emptyShared`. It is deep-linkable: `?tab=calendar&scope=shared&user=<id>` (the bell/batch link) — an explicit `?scope=` wins over the stored `daysOff.calendar.scope` pick when the caller may use it (the `dashboard.tab` idiom, never rewriting storage), picking in the Select drops `scope`/`user` from the URL again, and `user` marks the person's row (`aria-current="true"` on its name cell + an accent bar, scrolled into view). **Sharer side (D6)**: the single Share action is an icon in the grid's name cell, passed through `DaysOffMonthGrid`'s `renderRowAction` slot and rendered iff the row's server-computed `canShareCalendar` is true (the row header's accessible name stays the person's name only — `aria-labelledby` on the name line) (own row on "My teams", every row on the managed scopes, never on org/shared — gate on the flag, never on the scope); its aria names the person ("Share the days-off calendar of {name}", own row "Share my days-off calendar"), and ONE `ShareDialog` per page (`resourceType="DAYS_OFF_CALENDAR"`, the picked person's id) words its title/intro/withdraw-confirm for a calendar through the i18next context `shareKindContext()` (`utils/shareKinds.ts`; `dialogTitle_calendar` etc.). **Links (D7)**: the server `link` of a calendar share is the SHAREE's scope, so `shareOpenPath(share, viewerId)` (`utils/shareKinds.ts`) keeps it for the sharee only — the Shared screen's "Shared by me" tab and the activity log (viewer ≠ sharee) open the person's details page (`userDetailsLink`), or `/days-off?tab=calendar` (the Calendar tab) for one's own calendar; every other kind keeps its server link. **Activity rows**: a calendar share row rides the DAYS_OFF area (`activityDocumentKind` in `utils/describeActivity.ts` tells it from the area's person-scoped event rows by `SHARE_*`), is labelled by `documentLabel("DAYS_OFF_CALENDAR", details)` and worded with the `activity.shareNoun(Of).DAYS_OFF_named` nouns ("Shared Pat's days-off calendar with Ben"). Bell wording (`daysOffCalendar*` in `notifications.event.*`) rides `CALENDAR_SHARED_SPEC`/`CALENDAR_WITHDRAWN_SPEC`: the `self: "own"` param is a context variant combining with `until`. Tests: `DaysOff.test.tsx`, `DaysOffMonthGrid.test.tsx`, `utils/shareKinds.test.ts`, the calendar blocks of `Shares.test.tsx`/`ActivityLog.test.tsx`/`ShareDialog.test.tsx`/`describeActivity.test.ts`/`NotificationsButton.test.tsx`.

**Pulse results shares (v4.12.0, the ninth kind)**: the resource is a TEAM (its id is the team id) and there is no view page — the surface is the Pulse hub's Results tab. The "Shared with me" view (`view=shared`) lists the teams from `GET /shares?view=withMe&resourceType=PULSE_TEAM_RESULTS&status=ACTIVE` deduped by team; it is deep-linkable (`?tab=results&view=shared&team=<id>`, the bell/Shared-screen link — an explicit `?view=` wins over the stored `pulse.results.view`, the card of `team` is marked with `aria-current="true"` + an accent bar and scrolled into view, picking in the control drops both params). The sharer-side Share button sits in each own-view card's header, gated on the results DTO's `canShare` only (never `sharedBy`), and its dialog is worded by the `pulse` context of `shareKindContext()` (`dialogTitle_pulse` etc.). `sharedBy` renders as a dimmed "Shared by {name}" line on whichever view returns it; comments are fetched iff `canMonitor || canReadComments`; **the per-cycle fill-gate probe never uses a team shared with the caller** (a share-granted 200 is the sharer's gate, not the caller's — see "Sharing in the SPA" in `.claude/docs/features/pulse-surveys.md`); a lapse 403 (`utils/shareLapse.ts`) renders `pulse.results.sharedUnavailable` on the card. **Links**: the server `link` of a pulse share is the SHAREE's shared view, so `shareOpenPath` keeps it for the sharee only — the sharer, the team's manager and the activity log open `/pulse?tab=results&team=<id>` (no `view=`; the page picks the first own view holding the team).

Tests: `ShareDialog.test.tsx`, `api/shares.test.ts`, `Shares.test.tsx`, the sharing blocks of the seven view-page tests and of `NotificationsButton.test.tsx`.
